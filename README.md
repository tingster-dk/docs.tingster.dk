# Tingster Docs

**Your auctions, on your own website.** Tingster widgets put your live listings anywhere you
want them — your front page, a sidebar, a single featured lot — with one line of HTML.

### 📚 [docs.tingster.dk](https://docs.tingster.dk)

Everything you need to know about embedding Tingster lives there: the widgets, every option,
and live previews you can see running before you copy a thing.

---

```html
<script src="https://shop.tingster.dk/embed.js"
        data-tingster-widget="grid"
        data-seller="Your Company ApS"
        data-ref="your-site"></script>
```

That is the whole integration. No build step, no API key, no login, no cookies on your
visitors. Paste it where you want the widget, and it appears there.

## Three ways to show your lots

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **`grid`** | A responsive grid of your listings. Fills its container, grows to fit. |
| **`swipe`** | An accept/decline card stack, sized for a sidebar or ad slot. |
| **`card`** | One specific lot, featured on its own. |

[See all three running →](https://docs.tingster.dk/widgets/variants/)

## Getting started as a seller

**Already selling on Tingster?** You have everything you need. Put your **public seller
name** — exactly as it appears on your Tingster seller page, spaces and capitalisation
included — in `data-seller`, and pick any short label you like for `data-ref` so you can tell
your own traffic apart. That's it.

**Not on Tingster yet?** We take on auction houses directly — write to
**[kontakt@tingster.dk](mailto:kontakt@tingster.dk)** and we'll get you set up.
